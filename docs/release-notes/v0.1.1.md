**Test build — personal inventories for guests.** Every guest now has its own
inventory: press **Back on the guest's controller** and that player's own panel opens
on the left, with its own cursor, while pad 1's inventory keeps opening on the right.
Both (or all) panels can be open at the same time, and nothing pauses: monsters keep
fighting and every other player keeps walking, fighting and casting.

In the guest's inventory: stick/D-pad move the cursor, **A** wears an item (swapping
out the old one), takes it off, or uses a potion, elixir, book or scroll, **Y** moves
an item between pack and belt, **X** drops it, **B** or **Back** closes the panel.

Still from v0.1.0: start **Multi Player → Offline**, create a game, press any button on
pad 1, then press **Start on a second controller** to join. Guest controls outside the
panel: stick/D-pad walk, A attack or pick up, X cast, Y next spell, B drink a potion.
Full details and limits:
[docs/LOCAL-COOP.md](https://github.com/jarvis-jhk/diablo-splitscreen/blob/master/docs/LOCAL-COOP.md).

Known limits of this build: guests are **not saved**, have no character sheet,
spellbook or shop yet, cannot split gold or use cursor scrolls/oils, follow pad 1
between levels, and the camera follows pad 1. Tested with virtual controllers only —
please try it with real pads (and on the Deck) and report what feels wrong.

You need your own `DIABDAT.MPQ` (or `spawn.mpq` for shareware); no game data is included.
Linux and Steam Deck: unpack the `.tar.gz` and run `diablo-splitscreen/diablo-splitscreen.sh`
(it starts the bundled AppImage; needs glibc 2.38 or newer, e.g. current SteamOS or Ubuntu 24.04). Windows: unpack the zip and run `diablo-splitscreen\devilutionx.exe`.
This build sends an anonymous report (version, platform, "fatal error"/crash signal —
nothing else) if the game fails; set `DEVILUTIONX_NO_REPORT=1` to turn that off.
No automatic updates yet. Not an official DevilutionX release; based on DevilutionX
under its Sustainable Use License.
